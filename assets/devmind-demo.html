About the project

Built to turn scattered proof into a plan

Developers often have their work spread across GitHub and many practice sites, with no clear picture of what to improve. DevMind gathers that evidence, scores it and detects gaps.

The AI coach is given the same facts, so its advice names your real skills and numbers instead of generic tips.
